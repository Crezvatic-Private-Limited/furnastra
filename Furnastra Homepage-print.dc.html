05 — FURNASTRA + MITSU CHEM

A focused components brand, built on 35+ years of moulding.

Hospital furniture parts have been part of Mitsu Chem for years. Furnastra gives them their own name.

Mitsu Chem Plast has manufactured blow and injection moulded solutions since 1990 and is listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange. Furnastra is its dedicated healthcare furniture components business — same plants, same quality systems, with a team focused entirely on what OEMs need from a component partner.
